# V3.5.3 storyboard & continuity rulebook

Source: user-supplied 重要分镜.docx, SHA-256 27d7adcf3154362ef500851d1beccec811fb5088b56f08503feed4607620ae9f.

V3.5.3 adds mandatory per-shot continuity to V3.5.1 asset gate and V3.5.2 live-action style gate. It does not alter preapproved LOCK V2 actor identity or the BX05 director-approved CURRENT asset.

## Pre-generation G3-S checklist
- One source shot ID, source script, scene and temporal sequence.
- Verified image assets: scene, all appearing actors, props, animal, and style references; exact paths and SHA-256.
- Explicit lens/framing/axis, immutable spatial anchors, staging start/end and physical contact.
- Four concrete shot elements: visible scene, action, narrative/emotion, purpose in edit.
- Dialogue preserved verbatim; no dialogue represented by em dash.
- Action propagation: setup -> initiation -> impact -> reaction -> aftermath; direction and motion maintained into next shot.
- Lighting: key direction, color temperature, shadow/fill and material response.
- Camera move chosen for dramatic purpose, at most two composite moves; prefer <=180 degree orbit for multiple actors.
- Real performance: facial micro-actions and mouth synchronization without identity drift.
- No collage, extra cast, caption or fake approval label in delivered image.

## Modes
High-density short-drama mode: source recommends 15s independent segments, 1-3s shots, up to three additional inserts per segment, dramatic pressure/reversal beats. This mode applies only when chosen by the project.

Epic live-action mode (Eight Immortals): honor original 48-shot screenplay pacing and artistic long-take intent; **do not** force 15s segments, <=3s shots, or >=70s episode duration. Additional inserts require no invented plot events.

## Delivery
Fail closed: preflight of fields is not visual QC. Generate/edit a single frame; compare all identities and props to approved image anchors and verify temporal/physical continuity. Require G4 seven hard gates + G4-C photographic review + separate director approval before promotion. G5 independently checks video temporal consistency.
