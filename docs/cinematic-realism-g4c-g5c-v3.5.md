# OneFlow V3.5 | Cinematic realism, subtraction, and believable imperfection

Status: policy/documentation only; no claim of live V3.5 gate enforcement or visual pass.

## Priority
LOCK V2 Baxian mother-image hashes, approved costume and artifact groups > approved storyboard/scene contract > V3.0-V3.4 functional gates > V3.5 aesthetic preferences.
No new scars, costume distress, face shape, artifact colors, or iris edits may alter a locked source. Add environment imperfection or separately approved damage-state layers, never destructive mother-image edits.

## G4-C static cinema checklist (20 checks)
01 coherent single visual focal point; 02 visual subordination; 03 plausible imperfections; 04 approved aspect; 05 focal-length narrative purpose; 06 group-appropriate DOF; 07 action motion blur; 08 restrained film grain; 09 source-linked halation; 10 optical artifacts restrained or explicitly omitted; 11 palette follows locked clothing; 12 skin/environment light integration; 13 justified local saturation; 14 recoverable shadows and highlights; 15 physical eye catchlight; 16 coherent main/secondary lighting; 17 scale-appropriate texture; 18 physical force and artifact grip; 19 near/mid/far depth; 20 no pseudo-text, compositing halo, plastic skin.

## G5-C image-to-video
Use human-approved first-frame hash; screen whole clip plus first/middle/last and action/VFX/occlusion frames. Check stable grain, optical blooming that follows light sources, hair/fabric gravity and wind, motivated expression and physical environmental reactions, plus cross-shot color/exposure continuity.

## Important corrections to source suggestions
- Keep current Baxian 16:9 storyboard; 2.35:1 only with explicit reframing approval.
- 50mm is allowed, f/1.8-2.8 not mandatory: lens and DOF serve the actual shot.
- <=7 scene elements is a soft complexity guideline, not a license to delete one of eight approved immortals/artifact groups.
- Red halation, bloom, vignette, chromatic aberration and grain are optional, source-grounded film looks, not mandatory noise.
- No universal rule to make skin the very brightest region, limit saturation to 8%, or lift all black floors.
- A deliberate intermediate action plus physical environmental response is preferred for battle shots, not every cutaway.
